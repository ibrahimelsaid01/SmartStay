Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2024

01 Introduction

At Smart Stay, we believe that your privacy is the cornerstone of a premium hospitality experience. This Privacy Policy describes how Smart Stay Hospitality Group ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our platform, mobile application, and stay at our partner properties.

Our commitment to transparency means we only collect what is necessary to provide an exceptional stay, ensuring your data is handled with the highest degree of security and professional integrity.

02 Data Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us as well as data generated through your interaction with our smart hospitality ecosystem.

Personal Information

Name, email address, phone number, government-issued identification for check-in verification, and profile preferences.

Payment Data

Credit card details and billing addresses are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

IoT & Device Logs

To facilitate a seamless keyless entry system, we log smart lock activations (timestamp and method). We do not monitor in-room activity; logs are restricted to entry points for security purposes only.

03 How We Use Data
  • Service Fulfillment: Managing bookings, processing payments, and facilitating digital key access.
  • Personalization: Remembering your room temperature preferences and amenity choices for future stays.
  • Safety: Ensuring the security of our guests and properties through verified access logs.
04 Data Protection
"Smart Stay employs medical-grade security protocols to ensure your data remains your own."

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information:

  • AES-256 Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted at rest using industry-leading AES-256 standards.
  • Secure Server Storage: Data is hosted on isolated, private cloud clusters with 24/7 monitoring and biometric access control for physical hardware.
  • TLS 1.3: All data in transit between your device and our servers is protected by high-bit SSL/TLS encryption.
05 Your Rights

Regardless of your location, Smart Stay offers universal privacy rights to all its users:

Access

Request a digital copy of all personal data we hold about you.

Portability

Move your data to another service in a machine-readable format.

Erasure

Request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated logs.
